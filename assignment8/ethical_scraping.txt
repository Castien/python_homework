Are there specific rules for certain user agents?

The rest follow the general rules in robots.txt.


Reflect on why websites use robots.txt and write 2-3 sentences explaining its purpose and how it promotes ethical scraping.

Websites use robots.txt to communicate which parts of their websites automated crawlers should or should not access. 
It limits unnecessary traffic, protects server resources, and prevents bots from repeatedly requesting pages that have nothing to use.
It promotes ethical scraping by providing clear rules and guidelines for access, to avoid flooding websites with excessive requests.
Responsible web-scrapers should only collect information that is relevant and necessary, and end sessions appropriately.
